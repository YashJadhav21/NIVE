Every logo PNG has 3000 px on its longest side. Transparent exports contain
real alpha. Files labelled ivory panel, reverse logo and Instagram have an
intentional solid background. Extras/reverse_transparent.png is the transparent
ivory variant. Preview and QA sheets are not logo exports.

SVGs are genuine editable paths, with no raster logo, live fonts or external
dependencies. Lettering was fitted to the supplied raster silhouette; it is
not claimed to be original Cormorant font outlines or a pixel-identical trace.
Descriptor uses outlined DM Sans Medium. The N is derived from the approved
wordmark, not the ornamental board monogram.

Print PDFs contain scalable vector artwork and generic process-CMYK colour.
They are not certified PDF/X and carry no press-specific ICC output intent,
bleed, spot-ink plates or package dieline. A printer must confirm colour and
hairline reproduction on the selected stock. Read 14_logo_usage_notes.pdf.

Originals preserved byte-for-byte in Reference/. Hashes and checks in QA/.
No direct Figma/Illustrator import, physical press proof or trademark check.
